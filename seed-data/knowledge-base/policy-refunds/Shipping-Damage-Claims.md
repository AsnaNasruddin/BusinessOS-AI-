# Acme Robotics — Shipping Damage Claims

*Knowledge base: Policy & Refunds*

## Filing a Claim

To file a damage claim, attach photos of the packaging and unit. Claims must
be submitted within the 30-day window from delivery date. Specifically, we
need:

1. A photo of the shipping box, showing any visible crushing, tearing, or
   water damage.
2. A photo of the internal packaging (foam inserts) as received.
3. One or more photos of the unit itself, showing the damaged area clearly.
4. The order number or the email address used at checkout.

Claims missing photo evidence should not be auto-approved — ask the customer
for photos before proceeding, even if their written description sounds
credible. This protects against both carrier disputes and, occasionally,
fraud.

## What Counts as Shipping Damage vs. a Warranty Claim

Shipping damage is physical: dents, cracked housing, a shattered camera lens,
visible impact marks — anything consistent with the package being dropped or
crushed in transit. If the unit looks physically intact but doesn't power on
or a sensor doesn't work, that's a warranty matter (`Warranty-Terms.md`), not
a shipping damage claim, even if it's within the same 30-day window. The
resolution path differs: shipping damage claims skip the warranty diagnostic
step entirely and go straight to refund-or-replacement.

## Resolution

Once photos are verified against this checklist, the claim follows the
standard damaged-item terms in `Returns-Policy.md`: full refund or free
replacement, no return shipping required. Replacement is the faster path
when stock allows (ships within 2 business days); refunds to the original
payment method take 5–10 business days to appear.

## Carrier Disputes

Acme handles the claim directly with the customer regardless of which
carrier delivered the package — customers should never be told to "contact
the shipping carrier" themselves. Acme's logistics team handles carrier
reimbursement separately and invisibly to the customer.
