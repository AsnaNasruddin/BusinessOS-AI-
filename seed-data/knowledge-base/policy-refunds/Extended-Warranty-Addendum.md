# Acme Robotics — Extended Warranty Addendum

*Knowledge base: Policy & Refunds*

## What It Is

An optional paid add-on, purchasable at checkout or within 30 days of
delivery, that extends the standard 1-year hardware warranty
(`Warranty-Terms.md`) to **2 years total** from the delivery date.

## What's Different From the Standard Warranty

Everything in `Warranty-Terms.md` applies, plus:

- **One free accidental-damage replacement.** Covers a single incident of
  drop damage, liquid exposure beyond the IPX4 rating, or pet damage — the
  exact things the standard warranty explicitly excludes. Only available
  once per unit for the life of the addendum.
- **Priority diagnostic queue.** Self-test results submitted by Extended
  Warranty customers are reviewed same-day rather than the standard 2–3
  business day turnaround.
- **No consumables cutoff.** The standard warranty excludes brushes, filters,
  and wheels after 90 days; Extended Warranty customers get one replacement
  set of consumables at the 1-year mark at no charge.

## Checking Whether an Account Has It

The companion account record (visible to support, not covered in this
knowledge base's document set) shows an `extended_warranty: true/false` flag
against the order. Do not assume a customer has purchased it just because
they ask about it — confirm against the account before applying these terms
instead of the standard warranty.

## What It Does Not Cover

- Loss or theft.
- Damage from unauthorized repair attempts (opening the unit voids both the
  standard and extended warranty).
- The accidental-damage benefit, once used, does not renew or reset — a
  second incident falls back to the out-of-warranty repair fee described in
  `Warranty-Terms.md`.
