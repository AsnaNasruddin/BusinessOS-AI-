# Acme Robotics — Warranty Terms

*Knowledge base: Policy & Refunds*

## Standard Warranty

Every Model X200 and X200 Pro ships with a **1-year limited hardware
warranty** from the date of delivery, covering:

- Motor and drivetrain failure under normal household use
- Battery capacity dropping below 50% of original rated capacity within the
  warranty period
- Sensor or camera module failure not caused by physical damage
- Manufacturing defects in the charging dock

The warranty does **not** cover:

- Physical damage from drops, water exposure beyond the IPX4 rating, or pet
  damage
- Cosmetic wear (scuffs, scratches) that doesn't affect function
- Damage from third-party accessories or unauthorized repairs
- Normal consumable wear — brushes, filters, and wheels are considered
  consumables and are not covered past 90 days

## Filing a Warranty Claim

Support should ask for the unit's serial number (printed on the underside,
also visible in the companion app under Settings → Device Info) and a
description of the fault before proceeding. Warranty claims within the first
90 days can usually be resolved with a replacement unit shipped same-week;
claims between 90 days and 1 year go through a diagnostic step first — ask
the customer to run the built-in self-test (Settings → Diagnostics → Run
Self-Test) and share the results code.

## Extended Warranty

Customers who purchased the Extended Warranty Addendum at checkout (or within
30 days of delivery) get coverage extended to **2 years total**, plus one
free accidental-damage replacement in that window. See
`Extended-Warranty-Addendum.md` for full terms — this is a separate,
purchased add-on and should not be assumed unless the account shows it.

## Out-of-Warranty Support

Units past their warranty window (and without the Extended Warranty
Addendum) can still be repaired for a flat diagnostic + repair fee. Support
should quote this rather than offer a free replacement — routing an
out-of-warranty request through the standard refund flow is a common
mistake; check the purchase date against the warranty terms above before
drafting any refund-flavored response.
