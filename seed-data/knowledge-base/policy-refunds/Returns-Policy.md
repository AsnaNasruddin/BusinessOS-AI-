# Acme Robotics — Returns & Refunds Policy

*Knowledge base: Policy & Refunds · Last reviewed: internal use, fictional demo content*

## Standard Return Window

Customers may return any Acme Robotics product within **30 days of delivery** for
a full refund to the original payment method, provided the unit is returned in
its original packaging with all included accessories (charging dock, power
cable, filters).

Returns initiated after the 30-day window are handled on a case-by-case basis
by a support manager and are not eligible for automatic processing.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Items arriving damaged are eligible for a full refund or free replacement
within 30 days of delivery, no return shipping required for verified damage
claims. "Verified" means the customer has supplied photos of the shipping
box and the unit itself — see `Shipping-Damage-Claims.md` for the exact
submission process.

If a unit is defective but arrived undamaged (a manufacturing fault
discovered during setup or normal use), the same 30-day window and
no-return-shipping terms apply, provided the customer describes the fault in
enough detail for support to distinguish it from user error. When in doubt,
route to a human agent rather than auto-approving.

## Refund Approval Thresholds

- Refunds **at or below $500**: the support AI agent may approve and send the
  refund confirmation automatically once the claim is verified against this
  policy.
- Refunds **above $500**: require a manager's approval before the customer is
  notified. This is a hard rule, not a suggestion — do not draft a
  confirmation email implying the refund is final before approval is granted.

## How Refunds Are Issued

- Original payment method, when possible (5–10 business days to appear).
- Store credit, if the customer prefers or the original payment method is no
  longer valid — issued instantly.
- Replacement unit instead of a refund, if the customer prefers and stock is
  available — ships within 2 business days of approval.

## What's Excluded

See `Refund-Exceptions.md` for custom-configured units, clearance items, and
other carve-outs from this policy.
